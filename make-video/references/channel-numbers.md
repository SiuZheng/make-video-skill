## How to read Studio

- **Views** count every time a Short starts playing, even when someone swipes away after half a second.
- **Engaged views** count the people who stayed past the first seconds. They're the real audience.
- **Unique viewers** is an estimate that lags a few days, so ignore it on a new Short.
- **The number that decides whether YouTube keeps pushing a Short** is in Analytics → Engagement → "Stayed
  to watch vs Swiped away". YouTube first shows every Short to a test group (about <N> views for this
  channel, as of <date>). It spreads further only if enough people stay. Creators use about 70%+ as a rule
  of thumb; YouTube publishes no cutoff.
- **Timing:** judge a Short after a few days, not on its first day.
